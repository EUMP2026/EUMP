**Fig. 3 | Interpretation of 1,536-dimensional EUMP embeddings.** **a**, Absolute Pearson correlation coefficient (|*r*|) between each urban variable and its training-selected best-correlating embedding dimension, evaluated in held-out test cities. For dynamic variables, circles and error bars denote the mean ± s.d. across available years, with the number of years indicated above each point. Diamonds denote static variables. Dimension labels show the embedding dimensions with the highest training-set |*r*| in each available year, ordered by their frequency of occurrence. The x-axis variables are normalized difference vegetation index (NDVI), nighttime lights (NL), population density (POPden), total population (POPtot), impervious surface area (ISA), gross domestic product density (GDPden), total gross domestic product (GDPtot), electricity-consumption density (Elecden), total electricity consumption (Electot), background air temperature (Tabk), precipitation (Precipbk), wind speed (Windbk), building fraction (BF), building height (BH), elevation and urban area (Area). **b,c**, Spatial distributions among held-out test cities (*n* = 162) of four urban-environment clusters derived from NDVI, NL and POPden (**b**) and from the 30 embedding dimensions most strongly correlated with these variables (**c**). **d,e**, Spatial distributions among held-out test cities (*n* = 161; one city with missing BH data was excluded) of four urban-form clusters derived from BF, mean BH and the quartile coefficient of dispersion of BH (**d**) and from the 27 embedding dimensions most strongly correlated with these variables (**e**). For both typologies, embedding-dimension selection, k-means fitting and alignment of embedding-based labels with variable-based labels were performed using the training cities. The fitted cluster centroids were then applied to the held-out test cities shown in **b–e**. OA denotes overall accuracy, calculated as the proportion of held-out cities assigned to matching variable-based and embedding-based clusters after label alignment.
